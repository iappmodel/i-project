Destination
Three exits, one screen
Bank (1–2 days, no fee), debit card (instant, small fee), external wallet (xcoins required, Tier 4 gate). Slow is free, fast costs a little, external is earned. The hierarchy is deliberate.
Fee logic
Instant costs — slow is free
Bank withdrawals carry no platform fee. Instant debit card charges 1.5%. This is the real cost of instant settlement passed through honestly. Users who plan ahead pay nothing.
External wallet
xcoins gate the exit
External crypto or partner wallet withdrawal requires xcoins and trust Tier 4. The lock protects the pool from being drained by low-trust actors who haven't proven longevity on the platform.
Amount input
Max is the most common action
The percentage shortcuts mean users never need to type a number. The large numeric input is there for precision — but most users will tap Max or 50% and be done in two taps.
