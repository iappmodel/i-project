Zero cut
Tips are sacred — 100% to creator
The green bar at the bottom isn't just UI — it's a promise. Every tip reaches the creator whole. The platform earns from attention, not from human generosity. This distinction matters enormously to creators choosing where to build.
Coin choice
bcoins for small, icoins for meaningful
bcoins are the default — frictionless micro-tips that feel like a thumbs up with value. mcoins unlock content. icoins are a statement — tipping 1 icoin means something. The coin type signals the intent of the sender.
Note field
A message with money attached
The note turns a transaction into a moment. "This video changed how I think about X" plus 100 bcoins is a completely different thing than just 100 bcoins. The note field costs nothing to build and creates enormous creator loyalty.
Presets
Six amounts, two taps maximum
The preset grid covers the full range of tip intent — from a casual 10 bcoins to a meaningful 1 icoin. Most users will tap a preset and send. The custom input exists for edge cases, not for everyday use.
