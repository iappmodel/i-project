Tap to pay
NFC — same gesture as Apple Pay
In-person payments use NFC. The user sets an amount, taps activate, holds to the terminal. Any merchant with a [ i ] terminal or app accepts it. The gesture is already muscle memory from contactless cards — zero re-learning required.
QR code
Offline-capable, expires in 5 min
QR codes work without merchant infrastructure — a street vendor, a market stall, a friend splitting dinner. Each code is single-use and time-limited. The 5-minute expiry prevents replay attacks without adding friction to honest payments.
Pay link
The virtual payment primitive
A URL that pays. Shareable anywhere — messages, emails, social bios, website buttons. A creator puts iapp.pay/maya/10i in their Instagram bio and fans pay directly. This is the viral growth vector for merchant adoption.
Coin choice
icoins for real purchases, mcoins for content
Merchants price in icoins — the cash-equivalent. mcoins work peer-to-peer within the ecosystem. bcoins for splits and micro-purchases. The coin type the payer chooses converts automatically to what the receiver holds.
