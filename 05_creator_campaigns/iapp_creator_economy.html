Two sides of every content moment
User side
Every second of genuine attention generates value
1
Opens the app — enters an attention session. Timer starts on verified dwell.
2
Watches content — earns acoins per minute. Multipliers apply for focus, streaks, featured hours.
3
Engages actively — tips, shares, comments. Each action burns a small amount of mcoins or bcoins back into the creator pool.
4
Converts earnings — acoins → rcoins → icoins or mcoins via the reward pool.
5
Spends or withdraws — mcoins fund more content; icoins can be withdrawn as real value.
Creator side
Attention on your content pays directly into your wallet
1
Publishes content — sets a content tier (free, premium, gated). Each tier affects earnings rate.
2
Attention accrues — every verified minute a user spends on your content mints creator-acoins into your wallet automatically.
3
Tips received — user bcoins and mcoins spent on engagement land directly in your pool with zero platform cut.
4
Converts earnings — same pool mechanics as users but with a creator multiplier based on audience quality, not just size.
5
Grows reach — higher earnings unlock promoted slots, discovery boosts, and collab features.
