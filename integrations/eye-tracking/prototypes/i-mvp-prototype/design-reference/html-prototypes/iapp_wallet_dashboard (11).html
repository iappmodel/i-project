The three fintech questions
How much do I have?
Section A answers this immediately — total balance, coin split, pending, cash value in USD. The user knows their state before they scroll.
What can I do with it?
Section B gives six actions in two taps maximum. The actions are ordered by frequency of use — Withdraw and Convert first, Add method last.
What should I do next?
Section F answers this with personalized signals — best offer type, nearby GPS opportunity, tier progression timeline. Not generic tips. Specific, actionable, time-bound.

D · Activity
Four tabs, one ledger
Earned, Pending, Failed, Payouts. The Failed tab is the most important trust moment — users who see a transparent failed verification explanation stay. Users who see a silent balance discrepancy leave.

Payout blockers surfaced early
Identity, bank, tax form, limits — shown at rest, not at the moment of withdrawal. W-9 needed is visible before the user tries to cash out $100 and hits a wall. Friction at the moment of intent is trust destruction.
