How it works
Content you own on other platforms gets imported to your [ i ] board automatically.
You earn attention coins when users on [ i ] watch your imported content.
Disconnecting a platform hides its content from your board — nothing is deleted.
Connected strip
Active platform count at a glance
The green strip shows how many platforms are live and how many posts are in the board. It makes the user feel their content footprint is growing — a productive asset, not just a settings page.
Syncing state
Live sync is visible, not hidden
Instagram shows a spinning ring while syncing. The user can see it working in real time. Hiding sync states causes anxiety — showing them builds confidence that the system is doing what it promised.
Toggle mechanic
Connect and disconnect in one tap
The toggle is the primary action — not a button that navigates to an auth flow. Tapping a disconnected platform triggers OAuth in a sheet. Tapping a connected one offers to disconnect with a single confirm. Fast, reversible, no dead ends.
Content count
Posts are assets, not just content
Each connected platform shows a post count badge. "47 videos" frames the user's YouTube library as inventory — a board that earns passive attention while they sleep. The number makes the value tangible.
Info card
Ownership is stated, not assumed
The three-line how-it-works card clarifies ownership immediately: your content, your earnings, reversible at any time. This pre-empts the anxiety every user has about connecting a social account to a new platform.
