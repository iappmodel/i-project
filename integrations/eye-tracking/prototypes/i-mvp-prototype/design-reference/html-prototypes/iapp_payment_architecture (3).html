Security layer
Dual confirmation
Coins never move until both sender and receiver confirm. A 30-second window where either party can cancel with zero penalty.
Biometric auth
Every payment over 50 icoins requires Face ID or fingerprint. Under 50 — tap and go. The threshold is user-configurable.
Anomaly detection
Unusual spend patterns — new merchant, foreign location, off-hours — trigger a soft pause and in-app confirmation before coins move.
30-sec reversal
Any payment can be reversed within 30 seconds of confirmation, no questions asked. After 30 seconds, reversal requires merchant cooperation.
System rules
Verified attention only. Brands pay only for seconds a real human demonstrably watched. Bot traffic, background play, and inactive screens do not bill. This is the core promise to brands.
No competitor adjacency. Brand content never appears beside a direct competitor's content in the same session. Brands can specify exclusion categories at campaign setup.
ocoins are brand-locked. Offer coins issued by Brand A cannot be spent at Brand B. They are not general currency — they are targeted conversion instruments with built-in intent.
Transparency is non-negotiable. Every piece of brand content is labelled. Users always know when they are watching a sponsored moment. Trust is the product — deception destroys it.
Budget burns cleanly. Unspent vcoins from a campaign are returned to the brand at close, minus a 2% platform processing fee. No dark spend, no auto-renewal without confirmation.
The pay link is the viral primitive. Every iapp.pay/handle/amount URL is a shareable payment request that works without the payer installing the app. This is how merchant adoption spreads organically.
