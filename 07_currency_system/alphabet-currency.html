Coins in the Ecosystem
◆ Tier 1 — Core Value

The Foundation

These are the primary currencies that anchor the entire economy. Every user interacts with these from day one.

A
aCoins
Attention
The foundational earning currency. You earn aCoins by giving your attention — watching content, engaging with posts, completing view sessions. Attention is the raw material of the [ i ] economy. aCoins are the first thing every user earns and the primary input into the reward conversion pipeline. Earned passively through verified watch time, actively through engagement signals like dwell time, scroll velocity, and interaction depth.
Earnable Convertible → rCoins Day 1 Unlock Soft-Capped Daily
I
iCoins
Individual / Identity
The platform's hard currency — your personal value store. iCoins represent real, withdrawable value tied to USD. They sit at the center of the economy as the coin you actually cash out, tip with, subscribe with, and transact with. Earned through the rCoins conversion pipeline (never directly from engagement). The wheel mechanic: scroll down to claim iCoins as personal, inward energy. The coin that makes the [ i ] in [ i ] app literal.
Via Conversion Spendable Day 1 Unlock Cashable
V
vCoins
Victory / Value / Vision
The platform's social energy currency — collective, outward, aspirational. vCoins (also ViCoins) represent the community-facing economy. You earn them by giving upward energy through the wheel mechanic (scroll up). Used for boosting content, tipping creators publicly, voting in community decisions, and powering social features. vCoins are the opposite polarity of iCoins: where iCoins are personal and inward, vCoins are communal and outward.
Via Wheel ↑ Spendable Day 1 Unlock Convertible ↔ iCoins
◆ Tier 2 — Earning

The Input Layer

These coins are earned through specific behaviors and actions. They flow into the rCoins reward pool before converting to spendable currency. No direct cash path — always through the pipeline.

E
eCoins
Engagement
Earned through active engagement beyond passive watching — commenting, sharing, saving, replying, reacting. Every meaningful interaction generates eCoins. The deeper the engagement (long comment vs quick emoji), the more eCoins generated. This is the coin that rewards people for contributing to the conversation, not just consuming.
Earnable Convertible → rCoins Week 1 Unlock
F
fCoins
Focus
The eye-tracking premium currency. fCoins are earned when the attention verification system confirms deep, sustained focus — high dwell time, consistent gaze, minimal distraction. Think of these as the "verified attention" bonus on top of regular aCoins. Particularly valuable during promotional content where advertisers pay for verified eyeballs. Multiplied by the 2× focus mode multiplier when activated.
Earnable Convertible → rCoins Week 2 Unlock Eye-Track Required
W
wCoins
Watch
Earned specifically from watch session completion. Where aCoins drip in passively as you watch, wCoins reward the commitment of finishing content — hitting the 70%+ threshold, completing full videos, binge-watching series. The session-completion currency. Bonus wCoins for watching creators on consecutive days (watch streaks).
Earnable Convertible → rCoins Day 1 Unlock
K
kCoins
Knowledge
Earned by engaging with educational, tutorial, or informational content. The platform's way of incentivizing learning and growth over mindless scrolling. kCoins are generated when users interact with content tagged as educational, complete learning modules, or engage in knowledge-sharing comments. They also reward quiz participation and course completions.
Earnable Convertible → rCoins Month 1 Unlock
S
sCoins
Streak
The consistency currency. sCoins accumulate through daily login streaks, consecutive watch sessions, and regular posting cadence. 7-day streak = 2× sCoins for the day. 30-day streak = massive bonus + badge. The longer the streak, the higher the multiplier. Breaking a streak resets the multiplier but not the total — you never lose what you already earned.
Earnable Convertible → rCoins Week 1 Unlock Multiplier-Based
◆ Tier 3 — Spending & Utility

The Output Layer

These coins are what you spend. Converted from the reward pool, they unlock features, boost content, purchase access, and power transactions.

B
bCoins
Boost
The promotional amplification currency. bCoins are spent to boost your content — increasing its visibility, pushing it into feeds, and getting it in front of more eyeballs. One-way conversion from iCoins (1 iCoin = 100 bCoins). Once converted to bCoins, they can only be spent on boosting. This is the platform's native ad system run by creators for creators. Burns permanently on use.
Spend-Only One-Way from iCoins Month 1 Unlock Burns on Use
M
mCoins
Market / Merch
The marketplace currency. mCoins are used in the in-app marketplace — purchasing digital goods, creator merch, exclusive content unlocks, profile themes, badges, stickers, and other digital collectibles. Converted from rCoins at a 60:1 rate. This creates a closed-loop economy for digital goods that doesn't require users to ever pull out their credit card.
Spendable Via rCoins (60:1) Week 2 Unlock
U
uCoins
Unlock
The access currency. uCoins are spent to unlock premium content — gated videos, exclusive creator posts, pay-per-view events, behind-the-scenes access, early releases. Converted from rCoins at a 50:1 rate. Creators set uCoin prices for their premium content. The platform takes 0% cut on uCoin unlocks to incentivize premium content creation.
Spendable Via rCoins (50:1) Month 1 Unlock
P
pCoins
Power / Premium
The premium subscription currency. pCoins power the subscription economy — fans spend pCoins monthly to subscribe to creators at three tiers (Fan / Super Fan / Patron). Subscribers get badges, exclusive content, early access, and DM access at higher tiers. Also used for premium platform features like advanced analytics, custom profiles, and priority support.
Spendable Via rCoins Month 1 Unlock Recurring
◆ Tier 4 — Social

The Connection Layer

These coins fuel relationships, community, and creator-audience connections. They measure and reward the human side of the platform.

C
cCoins
Creator
The creator economy currency. cCoins are earned exclusively by creators based on their content performance — views, engagement rate, audience quality (not just follower count). The 60/30/10 attention split (creator/user/platform) flows through cCoins. Four creator tiers with multipliers: Newcomer (1×), Rising (1.3×), Established (1.6×), Signature (2×). cCoins represent your creative labor's value.
Creator-Earnable Convertible → iCoins Creator Account Required
D
dCoins
Discovery
Earned when you discover and share content before it goes viral — the early adopter reward. If you engage with a piece of content when it has under 1K views and it later trends, you earn dCoins retroactively. This incentivizes exploring new creators and undiscovered content instead of only engaging with what's already popular. The tastemaker currency.
Earnable Convertible → rCoins Month 2 Unlock Retroactive
H
hCoins
Heart
The like currency. Every heart/like generates hCoins — split between the giver and the receiver. This is tied directly to the heart button mechanic already built. Unlike other coins, hCoins are a dual-reward: the person who likes earns +1 hCoin, and the creator receives +1 hCoin. This makes liking feel generous rather than empty. hCoins convert to both vCoins and iCoins at low rates, bridging both economies.
Dual-Earnable Convertible → rCoins Day 1 Unlock Giver + Receiver
L
lCoins
Loyalty
The long-term relationship currency. lCoins accrue when users maintain sustained engagement with specific creators over time — following for 30+ days and still watching regularly, subscribing for consecutive months, being part of a creator's core audience. lCoins are the measurement of real fandom, not fleeting follows. Higher lCoin balance = better subscriber perks and access.
Time-Earnable Non-Convertible Month 2 Unlock Relationship Score
N
nCoins
Network
The referral and growth currency. nCoins are earned by bringing new users to the platform — the highest-reward tier in the funnel model. Also earned through collaboration: co-created content, duets, cross-creator features, and community events. nCoins reward you for making the network larger and more connected. Referral tiers increase with the quality of referred users (active > passive).
Earnable Convertible → rCoins Week 2 Unlock Referral-Based
◆ Tier 5 — System & Governance

The Infrastructure Layer

These coins govern the platform's health, trust, moderation, and internal mechanics. Users don't always see these directly, but they power everything behind the scenes.

G
gCoins
Governance
The community governance token. gCoins give users voting power in platform decisions — feature requests, content policy changes, creator program rules, community guidelines updates. Earned by consistent positive participation and high trust scores. Cannot be bought or traded. This makes the platform community-governed over time. One gCoin = one vote. Never convertible to cash.
System Currency Never Convertible Month 3 Unlock Voting Power
T
tCoins
Trust
The trust score currency. Every account has a Trust Score (0–100) denominated in tCoins. Verified identity: +20. Consistent behavior: +15. Flagged action: −10. Abuse confirmed: −40. Long-term loyalty: +10/year. tCoins affect earning multipliers, cash-out speed, brand deal eligibility, and feature access. Never convertible — they are purely a reputation measurement. The immune system of the economy.
System Currency Never Convertible Always Active Reputation Score
R
rCoins
Reward
The central conversion hub. ALL earning coins (aCoins, eCoins, fCoins, wCoins, kCoins, sCoins, dCoins, hCoins, nCoins) must pass through the rCoins reward pool before converting to any spendable type. No direct earning-to-cash path exists. rCoins are the clearing house — they aggregate all earned value and then distribute it into iCoins, mCoins, uCoins, pCoins, or bCoins based on the user's conversion choices. The beating heart of the economy.
Conversion Hub → iCoins (100:1) / mCoins (60:1) / uCoins (50:1) Always Active
Q
qCoins
Quality
The content quality score currency. qCoins are a system-level metric assigned to every piece of content based on production value, originality, engagement-to-view ratio, and audience retention. High qCoin content gets algorithmic priority. Creators see their qCoin score as feedback on content quality. This prevents the platform from becoming a race to the bottom — quality always gets rewarded over clickbait.
System Currency Non-Convertible Auto-Assigned Content Metric
O
oCoins
Origin
The content provenance currency. oCoins track where content was originally created and posted. Content made natively on the [ i ] platform earns an oCoins "native" tag, which gives it 2× reward multipliers across the ecosystem. Imported content from Instagram/TikTok gets a lower oCoins score. This is the mechanic that makes native content always more valuable than cross-posted content — driving the migration funnel.
System Currency Non-Convertible Auto-Assigned Provenance Tracker
◆ Tier 6 — Special & Rare

The Prestige Layer

Limited, seasonal, or event-driven currencies that create scarcity, excitement, and FOMO. These appear and disappear. They can't be farmed. They're moments.

Z
zCoins
Zenith
The rarest coin in the ecosystem. zCoins are only awarded for extraordinary achievements — first content to hit 1M views, community milestones, platform anniversaries, winning seasonal competitions. They never convert to cash. Instead, zCoins unlock permanent prestige badges, unique profile effects, and access to the Zenith Lounge — an exclusive creator/community space. Pure status. Pure flex.
Never Convertible Ultra-Rare Achievement-Based Prestige Only
J
jCoins
Jubilee
The seasonal event currency. jCoins appear only during platform events — holiday celebrations, launch anniversaries, community challenges, creator festivals, and seasonal campaigns. They expire after the event window closes. What can you do with them? Exchange for limited-edition badges, event-exclusive merch, temporary profile effects, and entry into event-only creator competitions. Creates urgency and excitement.
Non-Convertible Seasonal Time-Limited Burns on Expiry
Y
yCoins
Yield
The passive income currency. yCoins represent earned yield on staked/held iCoins. If users choose to hold iCoins in their wallet rather than cashing out immediately, they accrue yCoins over time — a passive interest system that rewards patience and long-term commitment to the ecosystem. Higher trust tier = higher yield rate. This keeps currency circulating inside the platform instead of being immediately withdrawn.
Passive Earning Convertible → iCoins Month 3 Unlock Staking Reward
◆ Tier 7 — External Platform Engagement

The Bridge Layer

These coins power the cross-platform integration — the full engagement of Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and other external platforms. They track, reward, and incentivize the migration funnel from external to native.

X
xCoins
eXternal / eXchange
The cross-platform bridge currency. xCoins are generated when users engage with imported content from Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, or other connected platforms. They earn at a lower rate than native engagement (by design — the funnel pushes native). xCoins can convert to rCoins but at a reduced rate compared to aCoins. They also gate access to external chain features at Trust Tier 4 — meaning you must prove yourself before bridging economies.
External Bridge Convertible → rCoins (reduced) Day 1 Unlock Trust-Gated (Tier 4)
Ω
ωCoins
Omega — "O for Other platforms"
Assigned to the letter concept of "Other" — this coin tracks cross-platform reputation. When a creator imports content, their external engagement metrics (IG followers, TikTok views, YouTube subscribers) are converted into ωCoins as a starting reputation score on [ i ]. This gives established creators a head start but doesn't give them the full earning power of native trust. ωCoins decay over time unless the creator builds native engagement — forcing migration.
External Reputation Non-Convertible Import-Based Decaying

Instagram Integration

Imported IG content earns xCoins at 0.3× the native rate. Stories, Reels, and Posts each have different xCoin generation rates. IG followers convert to ωCoins at 1000:1 as initial reputation credit.

TikTok Integration

TikTok imports earn xCoins at 0.4× native (slightly higher due to video-first nature matching [ i ]'s core). TikTok view counts seed ωCoin reputation. Duet-style co-creation generates bonus nCoins.

YouTube Integration

Long-form YouTube imports earn xCoins based on watch-time completion within [ i ]. YouTube subscriber counts convert to ωCoins at 500:1 (valued higher due to deeper audience commitment).

Migration Incentive

Creators who shift from import-only to native posting get a one-time "Native First" bonus: 5× their average xCoin rate in pure aCoins. The carrot that drives the funnel from external to native.

The Conversion Flow

How coins move through the economy — from earning to spending to cashing out.

Primary Flow: Earning → Reward Pool → Spending

aCoins eCoins fCoins wCoins kCoins sCoins dCoins hCoins nCoins → rCoins (Reward Pool) → iCoins (100:1) mCoins (60:1) uCoins (50:1) pCoins bCoins (via iCoins)

External Flow: Cross-Platform → Reduced Rewards

IG / TikTok / YT Content → xCoins (0.3–0.4× rate) → rCoins (reduced conversion) → iCoins / mCoins / uCoins

Creator Flow: Content → Creator Economy

Native Content → cCoins (60/30/10 split) → iCoins (direct conversion) → Cash Out ($)

System Coins: Never Leave the Platform

tCoins (Trust) gCoins (Governance) qCoins (Quality) oCoins (Origin) lCoins (Loyalty) zCoins (Zenith) ωCoins (External Rep) ⊘ No conversion. Internal measurement only.

Governing Rules

The six immutable rules that keep the economy healthy.

Rule Description Why
Rule 1 All earning coins must pass through rCoins before converting to any spendable type Prevents direct earning-to-cash exploits. Creates a single control point for inflation management
Rule 2 tCoins, gCoins, qCoins, oCoins, and zCoins never convert to cash or other currencies System integrity. These measure trust, quality, and reputation — they can't be bought
Rule 3 xCoins gate external chain access at Trust Tier 4 only Prevents bad actors from gaming cross-platform bridges before proving trustworthiness
Rule 4 bCoins are one-way from iCoins and burn permanently on use Anti-inflation mechanism. Every boost removes currency from circulation
Rule 5 Coin supply is tied to ad revenue — no revenue, no new coins minted Prevents hyperinflation. The economy is backed by real money flowing in
Rule 6 Native content (high oCoins) always earns 2× what imported content earns The migration funnel. External platforms are recruitment tools, not the endgame

NOTE ON THE 27th SYMBOL

ωCoins (Omega) — assigned to the concept of "Other platforms." Since no natural English word starting with a unique unused letter could represent external platform reputation, the Omega symbol (Ω) was chosen. It means "the end" — as in, the end of needing other platforms. The last step before you're fully native.
