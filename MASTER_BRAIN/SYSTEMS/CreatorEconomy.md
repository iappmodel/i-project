**Classification:** Candidate

## One-sentence definition

The **Creator Economy** coordinates how creators publish content, run campaigns, receive tips, and earn from **verified** attention and engagement delivered to advertisers.

---

## Three participants (economic view)

| Participant | Earns from |
|-------------|------------|
| User (viewer) | Verified attention — Loop 1 |
| Creator | Engagement, tips, revenue share |
| Advertiser | Buys verified consumption |
