**Classification:** Canonical  
**Confidence:** High

- Provides verified attention
- Earns rewards, builds trust, accumulates attention portfolio
- **Evidence:** Feed screens, watch-verify flows, wallet UX across `app/` and eye-earn-sparkle-archive

- Earns via quality engagement, not follower count

## Advertiser

- Defines actions, rewards, verification requirements
- **Evidence:** Campaign mocks in demoState, studio campaign panels (IVAULT checkpoint), HTML prototypes in `05_creator_campaigns/`
