### Cost Optimization Strategies

1. **Provisional-first approach** — $1,500-3,000 per provisional vs $8,000-15,000 per non-provisional; buys 12 months to convert
2. **Family filing discounts** — Most patent firms offer 15-25% discount for multi-application families
3. **Micro-entity status** — If eligible, USPTO fees are reduced 80%
4. **Phased filing** — Prioritize P1 families; use trade secret for lower-value patents
5. **Combination applications** — Some inventions can be combined into fewer, broader applications
