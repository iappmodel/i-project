The pool is not instant by design
The 2-second clearing sequence shows all five steps — pool entry, multiplier, fee, output, ledger write. That brief pause makes the mechanics legible. Users understand exactly where their coins went and why the number is what it is.
Receipt
Every number explained — before and after
The receipt mirrors exactly what the convert screen showed before the tap. No new numbers. No rounding surprises. The pre-confirmation breakdown and the post-confirmation receipt are identical — trust is built by consistency, not by hiding the math.
Wallet badge
New absolute balance — not just the delta
The green strip states the new total balance, not just "+5 icoins." The user knows exactly where they stand. Delta is useful for the moment; absolute balance is useful for decisions.
Reference ID is the dispute anchor
Every conversion logs a permanent reference on both sides of the ledger. If a user ever questions a conversion, the ref links the acoins sent to the icoins received in one immutable record. No "I never received it" is possible with a verifiable on-platform ledger.
