Security layer
Dual confirmation
Coins never move until both sender and receiver confirm. A 30-second window where either party can cancel with zero penalty.
Biometric auth
Every payment over 50 icoins requires Face ID or fingerprint. Under 50 — tap and go. The threshold is user-configurable.
Anomaly detection
Unusual spend patterns — new merchant, foreign location, off-hours — trigger a soft pause and in-app confirmation before coins move.
30-sec reversal
Any payment can be reversed within 30 seconds of confirmation, no questions asked. After 30 seconds, reversal requires merchant cooperation.
