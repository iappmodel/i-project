The rules of the system
Attention is verified, not trusted. The app uses scroll velocity, dwell time, and interaction signals to distinguish real attention from passive background play.
There's a daily soft cap. Earning slows after ~200 acoins/day to prevent farming. The cap rises with your trust tier.
Quality over quantity. Watching one video fully earns more than skipping through ten. The algorithm rewards depth.
acoins don't expire — but multipliers do. Use featured hours and streak bonuses while they're live.
