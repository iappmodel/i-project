The three core product loops of the i app: Watch-Verify-Earn, Browse-Save-Return, and Balance-Convert-Use

Loop 1 · flagship
Watch → Verify → Earn
The money loop
1
Open Earn — marketplace of available offers
2
Tap offer — see reward, duration, requirements
3
Complete — watch, survey, or GPS check-in
4
Verify — 5-gate engine confirms genuine attention
5
Reward hits wallet — icoins or acoins credited
6
Cash out, convert, or spend — loop closes
Retention driver: every session has a payoff
Loop 2 · retention
Browse → Save → Return
The habit loop
1
Consume media — feed from all connected platforms
2
Save — creators, offers, content to a board
3
Notifications — relevant new offers from saved creators
4
Return — pick up a saved offer or continue watching
5
Earn again — loop feeds back into Loop 1
Retention driver: saved content pulls users back
Loop 3 · fintech
Balance → Convert → Use
The value loop
1
See wallet grow — balance visible at all times
2
Convert — acoins → icoins at trust-tier rate
3
Withdraw or spend — bank, card, NFC, pay link
4
Trust deepens — tier rises, limits expand
5
Habitual — wallet becomes primary fintech layer
Retention driver: trust score is sunk cost
Filter: if a feature does not strengthen one of these three loops, it is probably noise. Every screen, every interaction, every coin type maps to exactly one loop — or it ships without justification.
