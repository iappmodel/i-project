In-Profile

Your identity, not the creator's.

Concept

The Profile tab (In-Profile) holds the user's account — trust score, verification badges, vision settings, and withdrawal setup. Distinct from OUT-PROFILE (bottom-left chip showing the creator on current media).

MVP: `ImmersiveProfileSheet` glass overlay + full `ProfileScreen` escape hatch.

Interaction

Tap Profile in the 5-tab dock. Opens a glass In-Profile sheet over media — same Picture 2 shell, not a settings dashboard page first.

Glass sheet

Quick access: signed-in state, trust snapshot, and paths to Vision controls or Full profile. Keeps the user in the immersive phone while managing account.

Trust & verification

Trust score (simulated 72 in demo) gates payout speed, daily caps, and withdrawal limits. ID verification and eye-session badges unlock higher tiers per economy rules.

Settings & withdraw

Vision controls for gaze/blink remote. Full profile opens trust card, Elo companion, subscription (Stripe when live), and proof-layer dev paths. Withdraw setup requires ID at payout.

Elo vs OUT-PROFILE

Elo is the center companion membrane (user's AI presence). OUT-PROFILE is the creator chip on feed. In-Profile is the user's economic identity — three separate zones per design law.

Platform impact

Without trust-gated identity, the wallet is exploitable. Profile connects reputation to privileges — faster payouts, higher caps, campaign access — making [ i ] a marketplace, not a faucet.

Pros
  • Clear separation: creator (OUT-PROFILE) vs user (In-Profile) vs companion (Elo).
  • Trust score ties reputation to economic privileges.
  • Glass sheet preserves immersive UX (Picture 2 law).
  • ID + eye verification badges reduce fraud narrative risk.
  • Vision controls centralized for accessibility power users.
  • Withdraw setup gated — aligns with Source of Truth payout law.
  • Completes 5-tab dock investor series.

In-Profile is where [ i ] users become trusted participants — identity verified, settings managed, and withdrawal privileges earned — while OUT-PROFILE keeps creator context on the feed.
