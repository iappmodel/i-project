Scene · Attention detected

Verified attention becomes a usable signal. POP reads gaze, face presence, head stability, and dwell — processed on-device for privacy.

POP is not eye tracking alone — it is multimodal sensor fusion (POPS six-layer model).

Scene · Intent formed

POP separates accidental looking from intentional action. Stable dwell + blink coupling forms governed intent before any UI action fires.

Scene · Safety gate

High-risk actions require confirmation, not raw gaze. Five gates check presence, gaze, stability, duration, and fraud before financial actions execute.

Scene · Verified action

Verified engagement creates auditable value. Reward enters pending review — no client-side minting. Proof Packet v0 seals session evidence.

Scene · Wallet settlement

POP links attention proof to payout logic. Server scores layers async; pending moves to available when review passes.

Scene · Platform value

A new market layer for paid attention and controlled interaction. Advertisers pay for verified engagement, not blind impressions.

What it does
  • Detects attention using gaze, face, head stability, dwell, and timing
  • Converts stable intent into controlled UI actions
  • Adds safety gates before irreversible or financial actions
  • Creates verification evidence for paid campaigns
  • Routes qualified rewards to pending review or wallet release
  • Enables hands-free navigation and accessibility controls
Why it matters

Current platforms monetize attention without proving it. POP creates a measurable signal — advertisers pay for verified attention, users earn from real engagement, creators get campaign proof, and the platform holds a defensible interaction layer.

Architecture
Camera / Sensors → On-device extraction → Gaze + attention model → Intent engine → Safety gates → Verified action → Pending reward → Wallet settlement → Reporting
Pros
  • New monetization primitive: verified attention
  • Lower fraud than passive impressions
  • Hands-free accessibility layer
  • Stronger campaign reporting for advertisers
  • Built-in safety before risky actions
  • Wallet-native reward settlement
  • Creator-friendly value split (60/30/10)
  • Scales to commerce, local offers, remote control

POP is not just eye tracking. It is the transaction layer for attention — a proprietary way to verify engagement, price it, route it through campaigns, and settle it into a wallet.
