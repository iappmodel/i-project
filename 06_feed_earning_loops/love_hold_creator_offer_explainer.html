Hold Love

A like is emotion. A hold is economic intent.

Concept

Hold Love converts creator appreciation into a deliberate, reversible offer.

Interaction

Tap = social signal. Hold + roll = priced support.

Why it matters

It separates engagement from payment. The platform only treats extended intentional pressure as value transfer intent.

Trust layer

Release does not instantly charge. The offer enters review, validation, and user-edit time before creator wallet settlement.

Creator impact

Creators receive stronger monetization signals than likes: amount, intent, timing, and content context.

User impact

Users can express support without leaving the feed, while retaining control before settlement.

Platform impact

The platform monetizes verified intent, not passive attention. This creates a cleaner transaction layer between content, emotion, and money.
