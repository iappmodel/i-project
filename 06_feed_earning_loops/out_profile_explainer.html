OUT-PROFILE

The creator on this clip — not you, not Elo.

Concept

OUT-PROFILE is the bottom-left glass chip showing the creator on current media: avatar, name, location. Separate from ELO (center companion) and In-Profile tab (user account).

Canon: OUT_PROFILE_ENGINE.md · `OutProfileChip.tsx` · `outProfileEngine.ts`

On-media context

Every immersive card carries creator attribution. Sponsored clips show campaign context — viewers always know who funded the content and who produced it.

Tap action

Tap OUT-PROFILE resolves via `outProfileTapAction`: if `sponsoredOfferId` is set → open offer/consent/watch flow; else → creator feed (roadmap).

Sponsored routing

Demo creator RAFAELO links to Nike Pegasus 41 offer — routes into Loop 1 without breaking immersion. Same shell, same glass language.

Creator feed path

Organic clips route to creator catalog when no sponsor is attached. Live creator API, follow graph, and cross-post to saved items remain on roadmap.

Identity triad

ELO = user's AI presence. OUT-PROFILE = creator on media. In-Profile = user's trust and wallet identity. Conflating these breaks the immersive IA.

Platform impact

Creator attribution is supply-side infrastructure — it connects content to campaigns, tips, and revenue share without a separate creator app shell.

Pros
  • Persistent creator credit on every immersive clip.
  • One-tap path from attribution to sponsored earn.
  • Glass chip preserves Picture 2 — no dashboard chrome.
  • Clear separation from user identity (In-Profile) and companion (Elo).
  • Engine-driven tap routing — extensible for tips and DMs.
  • Supports 60/30/10 creator economics narrative.
  • Completes Picture 2 shell zone coverage for investors.
