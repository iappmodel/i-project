ELO

The feed shows content. ELO holds the relationship.

Concept

ELO is a living presence membrane — procedural glass face contours over full-bleed media. Not a static avatar, not an orb, not a chatbot card.

Implemented: EloPresenceLayer, voice wake, presence panel, personality stack (MVP).

Voice evoke

Opt-in activation: a non-blocking “Say ELO” hint appears. User taps to arm mic — no always-listening. Saying “ELO” manifests the membrane at center (~1.35s emergence).

Glass membrane

Semi-transparent frosted contour lines (jaw, brows, eyes, nose, lips) with soft radial fill — background visible through the membrane. ~62% width, centered over media.

Relationship

Returning users see “I'm here. Tap to talk.” First visit opens onboarding: “I heard you.” Tap membrane → glass presence panel with chat, recommendations, and personality settings.

Presence panel

Panel surfaces memories, permissions, recommendations, and stack editor — companion, mentor, muse modes. Config persists locally (`i-elo-presence-config-v1`).

POP boundary

ELO reacts to POP vision landmarks and proof status — glow states, status line — but does not bypass proof gates. Companion layer, not a reward shortcut.

Platform impact

ELO owns the center zone; OUT-PROFILE stays bottom-left for creators. This inverts typical social feeds: Human → Presence → Relationship, with economy and gestures on the periphery.

Pros
  • Relationship-first UX — not content-only scrolling.
  • Voice evoke is opt-in — privacy-respecting activation.
  • Glass membrane preserves immersive full-bleed media.
  • Personality stack — mentor, companion, operating modes.
  • POP-aware without bypassing verification gates.
  • Does not replace creator OUT-PROFILE — clear zone ownership.
  • Defensible companion layer competitors lack.
