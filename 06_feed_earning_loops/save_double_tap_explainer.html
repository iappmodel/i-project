Concept

On the default Like/Love preset, double_tap → save. One button, three speeds: tap like, double save, hold offer. Saves are retention — not wallet mutations.

Shipped: binding in `presets.ts` · `GestureButton` FSM · rebindable via CONTROLS.

Double-tap interaction

Two quick taps on the glass heart within the gesture window. Pointer capture keeps the feed still. Distinct from single tap (like toggle) — the FSM routes to the save action.

Immediate feedback

MVP immersive feed flashes Saved toast via `onActionMessage`. Bookmark badge on the heart confirms capture — no REWARD sheet, no coin debit.

Loop 2 vault

Feed teaser path writes to `loop2_saved_items_v1` localStorage and opens the Saved screen. Full immersive save→vault wiring is the next Loop 2 slice — roadmap-honest, not vapor.

Three speeds · one heart

Tap = social signal. Double = save for return. Hold = creator offer + wallet. Users learn one muscle memory; power users rebind everything in the gesture builder.

Engagement signal

Saves are high-intent engagement — stronger than passive watch for recommendation and future eCoin paths. Distinct from verified earn (REWARD pill) and paid tips (hold).

Platform impact

Loop 2 closes the attention loop: watch → save → return → spend/convert. iSAVE is the vault layer for creators, offers, and routes — double-tap is the in-feed capture affordance.
