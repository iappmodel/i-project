Controls

A fixed UI is a cage. A composable rail is a platform.

Concept

Controls is the entry point to the Gesture Button Builder — every overlay button on the watch surface is user-composable: tap, multi-tap, hold tiers, and four-way swipe map to actions.

Shipped in MVP: builder sheet + localStorage persistence on immersive feed.

Interaction

Tap = quick settings. Hold 1 second = open full builder. Runtime gestures (0.5s arm on heart) are separate from builder mode — two distinct interaction layers.

Builder

The builder presents preset cards for Like/Love, Message, Share, Controls, and blank slots. Users select a button and remap its gesture bindings without leaving the feed.

Rebind

Each button exposes a gesture map: tap, double-tap, triple-tap, hold, and swipe directions. Defaults ship for Like/Love (tap = like, hold = offer, swipe up/down = tip v/i).

Persistence

Bindings save to the device and persist across sessions. The feed restores user preferences on load — no server round-trip required for layout customization.

Accessibility & POP

Composable controls are the foundation for hands-free navigation, POP remote-control layers, and assistive UX — users map actions to the gestures they can perform.

Platform impact

Fixed gesture rails commoditize apps. A rebindable rail is a defensible interaction layer — power users stay, accessibility expands TAM, and future campaign actions plug into the same engine.

Pros
  • User-composable — not locked to one gesture map.
  • Presets ship defaults; power users rebind everything.
  • Builder persists locally — instant customization.
  • Separates runtime gestures from configuration mode.
  • Foundation for POP remote control and accessibility.
  • Same engine powers Like, Message, Share, and future buttons.
  • Differentiates [ i ] from fixed-gesture social apps.

Controls is the composability layer: the platform does not dictate how users interact — it gives them a builder, presets, and a rail that grows with the product.
