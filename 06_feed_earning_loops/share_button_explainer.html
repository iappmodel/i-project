Share

A view is private. A share is distribution.

Concept

Share is the platform's distribution layer — designed to spread media moments with creator credit, content context, and tracked reach.

Current MVP shows a demo share sheet; full attribution pipeline and referral rewards are roadmap.

Interaction

Tap = open share. One gesture from full-bleed media to a destination grid — no leaving the immersive shell.

Destinations

Link, message, social, and more — the production layer routes to native and external channels while preserving the [ i ] moment identity.

Context-aware links

Every shareable link carries creator, content reference, and timestamp. Recipients land in a verified context — not a generic URL.

Creator impact

Creators keep attribution on every share. Distribution becomes measurable proof — stronger than passive impressions for campaign reporting.

User impact

Users distribute content they care about without breaking immersion. Intentional share beats algorithmic repost — the platform treats it as a signal.

Platform impact

Share builds the distribution graph: who shared what, to whom, with what attribution. This is the on-ramp to referral economics (nCoins) and advertiser reach reporting.

Pros
  • Attributed distribution — creator credit travels with every link.
  • Context-rich shares beat anonymous reposts.
  • Native to the feed — no app-switch friction.
  • Tracked reach for creators and advertisers.
  • Foundation for referral rewards (nCoins roadmap).
  • Separates intentional distribution from passive scroll.
  • Glass share sheet preserves immersive design language.

Share is the distribution primitive: verified media moments spread with attribution — designed to connect reach, reporting, and referral value inside the attention marketplace.
