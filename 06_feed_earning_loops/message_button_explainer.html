Message

A like is a signal. A message is a relationship.

Concept

Message is the platform's creator-contact layer — designed to open direct communication from inside the immersive feed, without breaking the watch experience.

Production inbox and threaded DM are roadmap; this animation visualizes the design-law target.

Interaction

Tap = open contact. One gesture from full-bleed media to a compose surface — no navigation away from the content.

Context-aware

Every message carries the media moment: creator, timestamp, and content reference. The production layer routes context automatically — investors see why this beats generic DMs.

Trust layer

Messages are opt-in and viewer-verified. Creators receive higher-signal inbound than public comments — designed to reduce spam and low-quality outreach.

Creator impact

Creators get structured contact with amount, intent, timing, and content context — stronger monetization and collaboration signals than likes alone.

User impact

Users reach creators without leaving the feed. Quick-reply chips and context attachment make outreach feel native, not like switching apps.

Platform impact

The platform owns the creator–audience relationship graph. Message is the on-ramp to direct commerce, collab, and campaign follow-up — inside [ i ], not on external socials.

Pros
  • Context-rich contact — every message knows what the user was watching.
  • Native to the feed — no app-switch friction.
  • Opt-in, verified viewer model reduces spam vs open DMs.
  • Higher-signal inbound for creators than public comments.
  • Foundation for collab, sponsorship, and direct support flows.
  • Platform retains relationship data — defensible social-commerce layer.
  • Glass compose UI preserves immersive design language.

Message is the relationship layer: social signal becomes direct, context-aware contact — designed to route through verification before creator inbox settlement.
