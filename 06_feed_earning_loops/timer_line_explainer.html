TIMER

Session truth at a glance.

Concept

Full-width 2px progress line under the notch — Picture 2's top zone. Tracks media session progress without dashboard chrome. Always visible, never intrusive.

Shipped: `immersive-feed__timer` in `ImmersiveFeedScreen.tsx` + `gesture-buttons.css`.

Organic watch

On feed media, the line reflects playback progress — white fill advancing left to right. Users see how far into the clip they are without leaving immersion.

Earn window

On sponsored clips, the timer doubles as earn progress — amber fill synced with REWARD pill and attention session. Full watch duration required for campaign completion gate.

Skip pauses earn

Skipping or scrubbing pauses the earn timer per offer rules (`OfferDetailScreen`: "skipping pauses earn timer"). Anti-farm by design — passive scroll cannot mint rewards.

Completion

Line hits 100% — session duration recorded for Proof of Participation. UI feedback is indicative; POP async review determines final payout.

Verification link

Timer completion is one input to the 5-gate engine — paired with gaze, presence, and fraud signals. Duration alone never authorizes settlement.

Platform impact

The TIMER makes watch-to-earn legible — users see progress, advertisers see completion proof, and the platform ties session integrity to reward eligibility.
