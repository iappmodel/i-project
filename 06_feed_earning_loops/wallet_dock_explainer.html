Wallet

Attention earned becomes balance held.

Concept

The Wallet tab opens the Attention Wallet — the platform's economic center. It stores balances, pending earnings, restricted earnings, and transaction history per Source of Truth.

MVP: glass sheet overlay from immersive feed (`ImmersiveWalletSheet`); full wallet screen for convert/withdraw.

Interaction

Tap the Wallet tab in the 5-tab dock — or the top-left chip while watching. Wallet opens as a glass sheet over media, not a separate fintech dashboard.

Dual ledger

Icoin (attention-native), Vicoin (brand/value), and USD estimate shown together. Separate ledgers, one wallet — mutations via Edge Functions only.

Pending state

Rewards enter pending first — after watch/verify/review. Users see +50 ic pending until the 5-gate qualification engine clears release. No silent balance inflation.

Release

When verification passes, pending moves to available. The top chip increments; the user sees the earn land — Loop 1 completes.

Spend / convert / withdraw

Earn more returns to feed. Convert moves between coin types. Withdraw routes to bank, PayPal, or gift cards per trust tier — all server-governed.

Platform impact

The wallet is why attention has value. Viewers earn; creators and advertisers fund the pool; the platform holds 10%. Without a native wallet, this is just another feed app.

Pros
  • Attention-native economics — not ad revenue sharing alone.
  • Pending → available builds trust and reduces fraud.
  • Glass sheet preserves immersive UX (Picture 2 law).
  • Dual i/v ledger supports brand and cash-value flows.
  • Edge Function mutations — no client-side coin minting.
  • Trust tiers gate withdrawal speed and limits.
  • Closes Loop 1: Watch → Verify → Reward → Wallet.
