REWARD

01 · Transparent offer

When a user engages with sponsored or reward-bearing media, the REWARD pill surfaces the exact coin amount on offer — before commitment. No hidden thresholds. The viewer always knows what verified attention is worth on this piece of content.

02 · Dual-ledger clarity

The amount displayed uses the platform's mono coin notation (50ic = iCoins, attention-native currency). Sponsored campaigns may also surface vCoin offers. Separate ledgers, one glass pill — the user sees offered coins and projected earnings in real time.

03 · Engagement arc

As the user watches and interacts, an engagement ring rolls toward the offered amount. Depth beats skimming: the arc only completes when dwell, attention score, and completion criteria align with the campaign brief — not on passive scroll-past.

04 · Completion moment

When the arc closes, the pill highlights — a single unmistakable beat that the viewer met the interaction contract. The amount glows, then transitions. This is the "earned it" signal investors can see without reading a dashboard.

05 · POPS review gate

After completion, the pill enters Review. The platform's 5-gate qualification engine (device signal, dwell, attention score, completion event, fraud check) decides whether the full amount clears — full or nothing, never partial. Settlement runs server-side via Edge Functions, not client-side minting.

06 · Wallet settlement

On approval, the pill fades and value moves to the Attention Wallet — pending first, then available per trust tier. The feed stays immersive; economics happen in the glass layer, not a separate fintech shell.

Why this matters

  • Prices attention at the point of consumption — familiar feed UX, novel economics underneath
  • Transparent pre-commit offer reduces fraud disputes and increases sponsor trust
  • Server-verified payout (POPS + Edge Functions) — no client-side coin inflation
  • 30% of sponsored revenue funds the viewer reward pool — aligned creator / viewer / platform split
  • Non-intrusive glass overlay preserves immersion; reward never hijacks the media canvas
  • Maps directly to Loop 1: Watch → Verify → Reward → Wallet
