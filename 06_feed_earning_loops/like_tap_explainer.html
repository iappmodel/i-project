Like

One tap. No coin movement.

Concept

The top rail heart uses the Like/Love preset: tap = like by default. It toggles engagement on the current clip without opening wallet flows or REWARD review.

Shipped: `like-love` preset in `presets.ts` · `useContentLike` + `content_likes` table when live.

Tap interaction

Single tap on the glass heart. Pointer capture prevents feed scroll while gesturing. Visual feedback is immediate — ring, fill, count bump.

Toggle state

Like toggles on/off per content item. Tap again removes the like. No Edge Function call — unlike tips or offers, likes are lightweight persistence.

Persistence

Demo: local toggle + count. Production path: `useContentLike` writes to Supabase `content_likes` when `walletBackend === 'live'` and user is authenticated.

Tap vs Hold Love

Hold ≥500ms arms offer mode — swipe tips, review sheet, wallet debit. Tap and hold are separate gestures on the same control; see Hold Love explainer for the economic path.

Creator signal

Likes feed creator analytics and future recommendation loops — engagement quality without pay-to-like. Distinct from verified earn (REWARD pill) and paid tips (hold path).

Platform impact

Familiar social affordance on immersive media — users get TikTok-speed feedback while the platform keeps wallet mutations on governed paths only.
