Promo

Sponsored attention, user-chosen.

Concept

The Promo tab (legacy Earn) is the sponsor marketplace — briefs for watch-to-earn, geo check-ins, and campaign tasks. Same glass immersive language as Feed; not a separate ad network UI.

MVP: `EarnScreen` card list + Supabase `promotions`; full immersive promo feed and local map layer on roadmap.

Interaction

Tap Promo in the 5-tab dock — or swipe right from Feed (gaze remote: `promoFeed`). Opens sponsor briefs without leaving the phone shell.

Sponsor briefs

Each card shows brand, action type (Watch · Check-in · Campaign), and iCoin reward. Users opt in — no autoplay pre-roll. Daily promo view caps enforced server-side.

Offer detail

Selecting a brief reveals verification gates upfront: presence, dwell, gaze, completion, review. Start watch session enters the same immersive player as organic feed — with REWARD pill active.

Watch to earn

Timer line tracks session progress. Sponsored bar fills as attention is verified. Skipping pauses the earn timer. POP / gaze signals feed `validate-attention` Edge Function.

Pending-first

Rewards enter pending — not instant credit. `finalize_promotion_checkin_reward` and `issue-reward` atomic paths credit wallet only after gates pass. Fraud-resistant by design.

Platform impact

Promo is how brands pay for verified eyeballs. 60/30/10 split funds creators, viewers, and platform. Without Promo, [ i ] is entertainment — with it, attention becomes a marketplace.

Pros
  • User-opt-in sponsored actions — not forced pre-roll.
  • Verification gates reduce bot/farm abuse.
  • Same immersive shell as organic feed (Picture 2 law).
  • Geo check-ins extend earn beyond video (Supabase shipped).
  • Server-side promo caps and atomic reward issuance.
  • Clear 60/30/10 economics for investor narrative.
  • Investor path: Promo → Earn → Convert → Withdraw.
